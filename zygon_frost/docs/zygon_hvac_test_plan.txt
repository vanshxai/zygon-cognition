ZYGON: end-to-end test for an HVAC engineer
============================================
Start: dashboard http://localhost:8800 -> start "ZYGON chat" (and "Frost Lang" for repair questions). Open http://localhost:8890.
The screen never scrolls. It shows the latest question or result, with the sheet of noted values on the left.
To fix any value: say it again ("hours 12"). To abandon a job: type "cancel".

1. SERVICE INVOICE (exact money maths)
   Type: I want a service invoice
   Answer the questions in order:
     systems 3 | price of one system 1200 | hours 40 | rate 85
     materials, one line each:  copper pipe 10 4.5   then   refrigerant 2 60   then Enter on an empty line
     travel 150 | discount 5 | tax 8
   Expect: equipment 3,600.00 | labour 3,400.00 | materials 165.00 | travel 150.00 | subtotal 7,315.00 | discount -365.75 | tax 555.94 | TOTAL DUE 7,505.19
   Then type: hours 12     Expect: labour 1,020.00, subtotal 4,935.00, discount -246.75, tax 375.06, TOTAL DUE 5,063.31; the old value is kept in the hidden log.

2. HVAC SIZING
   Type: size an air conditioner for 48000 btu
   Press Enter for the default dT 20.
   Expect: 4 tons | 2,222.22 CFM | 14,068 W (sensible heat, standard air, stated as an assumption).

3. WAREHOUSE LAYOUT
   Type: design a warehouse    Answers: 60, 40, 9, Enter, Enter, Enter, Enter (defaults: pallet 1.2 x 1.0, level 1.8 m, aisles 35%)
   Expect: area 2,400 m2 | 4 levels | 1,300 pallets per level | 5,200 pallet positions.

4. A FORMULA FROM THE 333 (exact, with put-back check)
   Type: current through a resistor     Answers: 2 A, 5 ohm      Expect: V = 10 V, put-back check passed.
   Type: electrical power from voltage and current    Give 230 V and 10 A   Expect: 2300 W.
   Units are accepted: "12 kW" is read as 12000 W.

5. ENGG HAND-OFF
   Type: measure the cylinder in this point cloud    Expect: a pointer to Frost Engg (http://localhost:8870) for the scan itself.

6. REPAIR AND GENERAL QUESTIONS (Frost Lang)
   Type: my furnace keeps tripping the breaker    /    why is my AC not cooling
   KNOWN GAP: Lang has no HVAC repair lessons yet. Expect an honest "I do not know that yet" or a weak answer. Teach lessons next.

7. MEMORY
   Click "New chat", do job 2, then pick the first chat in "Earlier chats". Expect it to reopen with the same sheet and result.

8. WRONG ROUTE TEST (the router)
   Try 10 of your own phrasings of jobs 1-5 (site shorthand, typos). Write down every question that goes to the wrong place and what you expected.
   Reported accuracy so far: 79% to 83% on 47 questions written by the builder, not independent. Your list is the first honest score.

WHAT TO RECORD FOR EACH STEP: the exact words typed, what came back, right or wrong, and the time it took.
