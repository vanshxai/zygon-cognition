3  Where a reply stops: the directional gate

The best sentence a is the anchor. Walk forward along the book's path, one sentence j = 1, 2, … at a time: gj = e−(j−1)/τ · ( max(rj, cj) + β ) keep sentence a+j while gj ≥ θ, words ≤ 90, and the paragraph has not ended rj = idf share of the typed words in it   cj = idf share of the anchor's rare words in it. τ = 1, θ = 0.25, β = 0.4. A product of an exponential distance term and a match term: not a probability, nothing is normalised.
