Exponent store: what the PDF turned into, in 3D

VIEW

drag = rotate, wheel = zoom, click a dot = select, legend = show/hide a topic. Each dot is one sentence of the PDF; each curve is a "tangent shot" from a sentence to the sentence that follows it (its stored reply).

SELECTED SENTENCE

click a dot
the staircase: each word of the sentence as a point at its own angle theta (atan2 of its stored y, x) and radius, one step up per word

ASK THE STORE (no vector or matrix maths: looks words up, averages 3 numbers each, shoots to the nearest dots)
